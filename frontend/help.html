How do I place an order?

Choose your style, size and colour, then add it to your cart. Choose delivery and select Continue to order. When the backend catalogue is available, submit your details and select a configured payment option to place the order. If the catalogue is not available, you can send a request through WhatsApp; that message does not submit or reserve an order.

How much is delivery?

The cart displays delivery fees configured by the shop. The backend calculates the final fee when an order is submitted.

How long does it take?

Made-to-measure production is estimated at 7–14 business days, subject to confirmation. Delivery estimates are shown in the cart; confirm the schedule for your location and occasion with the team.

Can I pay on this website?

Bank transfer and pay on delivery are available when configured. Paystack is shown only when online payments are enabled. Never share your card PIN or one-time password with the shop.

Can I request an alteration, cancellation or return?

Contact the shop with your order details to discuss the issue. Confirm the applicable alteration, cancellation, return and refund terms before agreeing to an order, especially for custom garments. No standard return window is published here.

How do I choose a size?

Read our measurement guide and confirm fit with the team. Select Custom for made-to-measure requests.

Where is my order?

Sign in to view order history, or contact the shop on WhatsApp for help with an order.

Are my saved items linked to my account?

Your cart and wishlist are saved for this browser in local storage, independently of sign-in. They are not synchronized across devices. Clearing this browser's site data removes these saved selections.
