Browsing and orders

The backend catalogue and order service are authoritative for availability, product prices and delivery fees. Submitting an order creates an order request; payment remains pending until confirmed. When the backend catalogue is unavailable, the WhatsApp request option does not reserve stock or submit an order.

Custom garments

Made-to-measure work depends on confirmed designs and accurate measurements. Share labelled measurements and discuss fit before production. The indicative production estimate of 7–14 business days is subject to confirmation for your garment.

Read the measurement guide

Delivery and payment

Choose a delivery option to see an estimate. The backend calculates the applicable delivery fee. Available payment methods are shown during checkout; Paystack is offered only when the shop has enabled it. Confirm the production schedule and delivery arrangements with the shop.

Alterations, cancellation, returns and refunds

Contact the shop to confirm the terms applicable to your garment before agreeing to an order, particularly for made-to-measure items. If there is an issue, provide your order details and describe it in your existing WhatsApp conversation or through the contact form. No standard return period or refund entitlement is specified by this website; applicable statutory rights are unaffected.

Reviews and accounts

Submit only genuine experiences and avoid personal or sensitive information. Reviews are moderated before publication. Keep account credentials private. Carts and wishlists are saved for your browser and do not synchronize across accounts or devices.

Contact

Send an enquiry or contact the shop on WhatsApp for order help, legal business details or clarification of terms.
