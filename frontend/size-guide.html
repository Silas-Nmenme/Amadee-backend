A little care before we cut.

Our collection offers XS–XL and Custom. Fit varies by style and age; confirm your measurements with our team before ordering.

Ask about your fit

Before you measure

Use a soft measuring tape over light clothing. Stand naturally and keep the tape level and comfortably close without pulling it tight. Ask someone to help where possible.

Record measurements in centimetres and label each value. For a child's outfit, include their age and height and ask our team about room for growth.

Your measurement checklist

Bust / chest
Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level across your back.
Waist
Measure around your natural waist while standing comfortably.
Hips
Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, with feet together.
Garment length
Tell us the style and desired length. Ask our team where to start the measurement for that garment.

Choosing Custom

Select Custom on your chosen product, then include your labelled measurements in the cart's special requests field. Our team confirms fit and any additional measurements on WhatsApp before production.

We do not publish a universal size chart because measurements must be confirmed for the selected style. If you are between sizes, ask us before choosing.
