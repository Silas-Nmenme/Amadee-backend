Information you share

Contact details and messages submitted through the contact form are sent to the shop's backend service and may be saved so the shop can respond. Email addresses submitted for collection updates are sent to the shop. Account information is handled by Netlify Identity for sign-in, email confirmation, password recovery and profile management.

Reviews are stored for moderation. Approved reviews display your submitted name, rating, message and submission date publicly. Do not include private contact or payment information in a review.

Saved shopping selections

Your cart and wishlist are stored in this browser using local storage and are not linked to your account. Clearing this browser's site data removes these saved selections.

The site does not currently load optional analytics. Account sessions also use browser storage and cookies needed for authentication.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the storefront and provides account services through Netlify Identity. The backend API is hosted on Vercel and uses its configured database and email services to process contact messages, reviews and account-related requests. Account confirmation and recovery require email processing. When you continue an order on WhatsApp, your selected products and the details you enter are placed in a message for you to send; WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

No card payment is collected on this website. Do not send card PINs, passwords or one-time codes.

Contact and requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or to stop collection updates, contact the shop or message us on WhatsApp. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant request without sending sensitive documents.

Contact the shop for its legal business details and applicable retention periods. This page describes the website's current functionality; those business-specific details have not yet been published.
