Information you share

Contact details and messages submitted through the contact form are sent to the shop's backend service and may be saved so the shop can respond. Email addresses submitted for collection updates are sent to the shop. Account details are handled by the Amadee-Stiches backend for email verification, sign-in, password recovery and profile management.

Reviews are stored for moderation. Approved reviews display your submitted name, rating, message and submission date publicly. Do not include private contact or payment information in a review.

Order details submitted through checkout are sent to the backend and stored to process the order and provide order history. Online payments, when enabled, are processed by Paystack.

Saved shopping selections

Your cart and wishlist are stored in this browser using local storage and are not linked to your account. Clearing this browser's site data removes these saved selections.

The site does not currently load optional analytics. Account sessions use a secure HttpOnly cookie.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the storefront and proxies API requests to the backend hosted on Vercel. The backend uses its configured database and email services to process contact messages, reviews, orders and account-related requests. When you continue an order on WhatsApp, details you provide are included in a message for you to send; WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply.

When Paystack is enabled and selected, payment is completed on Paystack. Do not send card PINs, passwords or one-time codes to the shop.

Contact and requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or to stop collection updates, contact the shop or message us on WhatsApp. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant request without sending sensitive documents.

Contact the shop for its legal business details and applicable retention periods. This page describes the website's current functionality; those business-specific details have not yet been published.
